ABOUT US

Technology that makes elder care simpler

ElderCare Companion is a student-built full-stack solution focused on a real social problem: helping families organize everyday care for senior citizens.

The problem

Families may need to remember multiple medicines, doctor visits, health details and emergency contacts. Information can become scattered across notebooks, messages and phones.

Our solution

ElderCare Companion brings these everyday tasks into one easy-to-understand platform. The goal is not to replace doctors or emergency services, but to help families stay organized and respond faster.

Project goals

  • Make care information easier to organize.
  • Reduce missed medicine and appointment reminders.
  • Keep important health information accessible.
  • Provide a clear emergency-contact workflow.
  • Create a simple interface for seniors and caregivers.
